MeterBar test kit - FOR YOUR WINDOWS 11 PC (kit v0.18)
================================================

Yes, this is a Windows kit. If you saw the word "Linux" in
BUILD_LOG.md, that's just the machine Milo used to test the
meter math before packaging it for you. Ignore it.

DO THIS:
1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere (Desktop is fine).
2. Make sure Visual Studio 2022 is installed with the
   workload "Desktop development with C++".
3. Double-click build.bat
   - The black window MUST stay open when it finishes.
   - First run downloads JUCE from the internet (a few min).
   - Everything is also written to build-log.txt.
4. If it says FAILED: open build-log.txt and send Milo the
   error lines. Don't fight it, that's his job.
5. If it says DONE: double-click
   build-app\app\MeterBar_artefacts\Release\MeterBar.exe
   It starts on your DEFAULT output (whatever plays through
   your usual speakers/headphones), so play a song and all 6
   meters should move with no audio setup.

NEW IN v0.18: the bar can now RESERVE the top strip of your
monitor (toolbar "Reserve" button) so maximised windows stop
below it instead of sliding under it, plus Arrange/Restore
buttons that slide floating windows out from under the bar.
README_TEST.md leads with the click-by-click tests for it.

Then work through README_TEST.md (the click-by-click checklist)
and send Milo a screenshot of the bar.

SHARING IT (new in v0.15): to give MeterBar to a friend as a
one-click installer, double-click build-release.bat instead -
see the "Sharing it" section of README_TEST.md.
